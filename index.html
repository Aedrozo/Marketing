          One-time setup · 30 minutes

          Connect true auto-posting

          1. Convert your Instagram to a Professional account (Settings → Account type). Free, takes 2 minutes, unlocks scheduling and analytics.
          2. Link it to a Facebook Page in Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com). This is what allows software to post for you.
          3. Pick your scheduler: Meta Business Suite (free, official) or Buffer / Later / Metricool (nicer bulk tools). Connect Instagram when prompted.
          4. Export your month below and bulk-import it — every post lands pre-written, pre-timed, compliance-footed.
          5. Set up ManyChat keywords — every auto-reply script is written for you in the Leads & Email tab (GUIDE, READY, EQUITY, CREDIT and more). Paste each into ManyChat once and DM replies go out instantly, 24/7.
          Your weekly rhythm · under 1 hour

          The hands-off workflow

          1. Sunday (20 min): open the Calendar, review the week, export or queue the 7 posts.
          2. One filming block (30 min): batch-record the week's reels from the Reel Studio shot lists — 3-4 scripts in one sitting, same outfit trick: change shirts between scripts.
          3. Daily (10 min): run the story playbook, reply to comments and DMs. This is the one thing automation should never do — relationships are the product.
          4. Monthly (15 min): swap in fresh client stories and market numbers, check analytics, double down on what worked.
