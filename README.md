# Gem Marketing OS

**The complete marketing command center for Gem Home Team · NEO Home Loans, powered by Better.**

A fully self-contained web application that plans, writes, and compliance-checks your entire
social media presence — so your only jobs are filming ~30 minutes a week and answering DMs.

## How to open it

No installation, no server, no account.

1. Download or clone this repository
2. Double-click **`index.html`** — it opens in any browser
3. Go to **Settings**, enter your name and NMLS ID, hit Save

That's it. Everything the app generates from then on is personalized and carries your
compliance footer automatically. Your settings are saved privately in your own browser.

## What's inside

| Tab | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | Today's post, the next 7 days, and your daily story playbook |
| **Calendar** | Auto-generates every month using the top-producer cadence (4 reels, 1 carousel, 1 static, 1 engagement per week). Click any day to generate that post. CSV export. |
| **Content Studio** | 24 ready-to-post templates across 7 pillars — each with 3 hook options, full caption, CTA, hashtags, design direction, and compliance footer. One-click copy. |
| **Reel Studio** | 8 shot-by-shot reel scripts timed to the second: what to say, what to show, what text goes on screen, audio and cover-frame direction |
| **Compliance** | Screens any copy against NMLS advertising requirements, Reg Z (TILA) triggering terms, Reg N (MAP Rule), and UDAAP deceptive-claim rules — with plain-English explanations and suggested rewrites. Auto-adds missing disclosures. |
| **Hashtags** | 8 curated sets by content type, copy-ready |
| **Brand Kit** | Logo files, exact color system, typography rules, and the "top 1%" design principles — photography-first, editorial, professional |
| **Automation** | The 30-minute setup that connects true auto-posting (Meta Business Suite / Buffer / Later / Metricool), bulk CSV export of two months of finished posts, and the under-1-hour weekly workflow |

## The automation model

Instagram only allows software to post through an approved scheduler connected to a
Professional account — that's a platform rule. This system is built around it:

1. **It writes everything** — captions, hooks, CTAs, hashtags, disclosures
2. **It plans everything** — a full month scheduled to optimal posting times
3. **You export once a month** — one CSV bulk-import into your scheduler and every post goes out on time, automatically
4. **DM keywords run 24/7** — pair with ManyChat so GUIDE / READY / EQUITY / CREDIT requests get answered instantly

What stays human on purpose: your face on camera and your replies in the DMs.
Those are the two things that turn followers into closings.

## Compliance note

The Compliance Center is an automated guardrail based on common NMLS, Reg Z, Reg N, and
UDAAP advertising standards. It is **not legal advice** and does not replace review by your
company's compliance department. State advertising requirements vary.

## Tech

Plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript — zero dependencies, zero build step, works offline.
Content library lives in `js/data.js`; add your own topics, reel scripts, and hashtag sets there.
